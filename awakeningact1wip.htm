Chapter 0: Origins    

Before the universe, the ancients, or even the chaos emeralds, there was once a grand creature. She was Called, “The Mother”. She gave birth to two races, the Angles, and the Beasts. Both races had differences, but The Mother kept peace between them. until something happened, an unknown accident wich created a material called “the dark”.  

 It was poisonus to her. it caused her death.  

while the angels were immune to its effects, The beasts were not so lucky, instead of getting sick and dying though, it corrupted them, twisting their minds and Making them become detroyers of creation.  

The Angles sought to create and preserve life, while The Beasts became savage, hellbent on destruction, ending any life or creation they came across.   the angels knew the beasts must be stopped.    

  

so a war broke out, the angles and the beasts fought, and fought, and fought, until something 

happened, an explosion made by the angles. wich caused the universe to begin, and them to be almost wiped out. Almost…    

    

a remenant of both races were left, their war continued in the darkest corners of the universe

but one day, it all stopped, did they all give up ? or was something else going on ?    

one survivor, a female beast, discovered a planet, it was called “Mobius”. She went there, 

finding the creatures who lived there to be compatible. And so, She took the form of a female wolf and procreated with a male wolf. He was an archeologist, he found a ancient scriptures and an artifact. He translated them finding out about the beasts and the angels. The artifact was created by the angels to contain beasts. His wife showed fear towards it, she tried her best to hide. He began to become suspicious, the beasts secrets were slowly becoming more revealed. Though she didn’t know he knew, so he was safe, for now…  

then came a time came when she gave birth, but something was wrong, the child was a wolf,  and not fully a Beast as she expected, she wanted a full blooded beast to assist her in destruction, a wolf was useless to her. One night, she went to dispose of the child, trying to end its life as she saw it as worthless. She transformed into her beast form, the father saw it, he already suspected her being a beast. He used the artifact to lock her physical form away from this world, but sacrificed his own life. He saved the child, but the child was now alone.   and so, began the life of jack.

eggpawns, you put on the hood of your hoodie, theres something about you, a secret, you don’t know why, but you have powers, your hands project into giant claws, chains that you attack with. You rather stay anonymous, you destroy the eggpawns quickly and stealthily, no one sees you, at least the citizens, the robots do, and theres no chance of them surviving.    

    

You move away quickly from the scene, leaving something for the heroes to save, as you head to your home, a small cabin near the town, it was abandoned when you found it. You refined it a bit, a bed, somewhere to keep food and water, a way to get electricity, a cheap stove and a workable microwave, a vcr player with an old tv you found in the dump, some tapes, and that’s all you need really.    

“Home Sweet Home” you say to yourself as you look at your house.    

Its lunch time you take some food out, and warm it up. A kind of decently sized piece of meat, you sit down at the makeshift table and eat.    

Youre bored, you pick a random tape, put it in the vcr player, and start watching whatevers on the tape so you can pass the time. then, you do some chores, you might be done with the outside stuff in the morning, but the in house chores you usually leave for after lunch, and before dinner. you get done with them quick, then you make your dinner, eat it, and then go to bed. in your bed, your laying down, fidgeting a random piece of wood using your powers, you don’t know  where you got your powers, but at least you have SOMETHING interesting about you.    

Its getting late, you start to fall sleep, slowly falling asleep as your vision fades to dreams. Another day, passing by, without changes, or differences from the last day you had, or the one before that, or the one before that…  Your dream though, its different.  today, you see a figure, a beastly, female figure, standing in what looks like a burning,   

destroyed version of the planet (Mobius), shes smirking deviously, creepy. The morning light  awakens you. “that was a strange dream” you whisper to yourself. the day isn’t gonna wait for you to think about your dream though, and chores aren’t gonna do themselves, so you shrug it off, dismissing it as a random nightmare and move on with your day, But one question wont leave your mind “who was that figure, why did she seem so… familiar ?”
